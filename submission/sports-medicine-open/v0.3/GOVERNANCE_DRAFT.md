# OPRC Governance Draft v0.3

**Status:** Draft governance proposal for review; not yet ratified.

## 1. Scope

This document governs promotion of candidate OPRC artifacts toward stable releases. It does not grant OPRC authority over pickleball research and does not make current candidate artifacts standards.

## 2. Roles

### Maintainer

Maintains repository integrity, releases, and change records. The founder may act as maintainer during the candidate phase but should not hold unilateral authority over stable releases.

### Contributors

May propose additions, changes, crosswalks, translations, tests, or removals.

### Independent reviewers

Evaluate candidate changes and stable-release readiness. Reviewers should disclose relevant institutional, collaborative, financial, organizational, or intellectual-property relationships.

### Release panel

For stable releases, the proposed minimum is three independent reviewers from at least two institutions and two methodological/domain perspectives. This is a draft rule requiring external review before adoption.

## 3. Change proposal requirements

A change proposal should state:

- affected artifact and version;
- problem being solved;
- proposed definition or change;
- evidence or use case;
- expected burden;
- privacy or jurisdictional implications;
- compatibility consequences;
- relationship to external standards;
- migration/deprecation plan where applicable.

## 4. Decisions

A candidate component may be:

- retained;
- revised;
- moved from universal core to a module;
- replaced by or cross-walked to an external standard;
- split;
- deprecated;
- retired.

A decision should include a public rationale. Rejected proposals and unresolved objections should remain discoverable.

## 5. Stable-release guardrails

No v1.0 stable-standard claim should be made until all of the following have occurred:

1. structured review by independent external experts;
2. use or mapping by multiple independent datasets/sites;
3. documented ambiguity and burden testing;
4. versioned machine-readable definitions;
5. explicit crosswalks to relevant mature standards;
6. a release decision not controlled unilaterally by the founder;
7. persistent archival deposit and citation metadata.

## 6. Conflicts and founder dependence

The founder's authorship, maintenance role, organizational roles, and any financial or intellectual-property relationships relevant to OPRC should be disclosed in publications and release decisions. Stable governance should progressively reduce dependence on the founder for interpretation and approval.

## 7. Deprecation

Deprecated fields remain documented with:

- last valid version;
- reason for deprecation;
- replacement or migration guidance, if any;
- effective date;
- known compatibility impact.

Silent deletion is not permitted for stable releases.

## 8. Authorship and credit

Repository ownership does not determine scholarly authorship. Manuscript authorship should follow contribution and journal policy. Non-author contributions should be recognized through repository history, contributor metadata, acknowledgements, or formal contributor roles.

## 9. Current status

This governance document is itself a candidate. It has not yet been approved by an independent release panel and should not be represented as community governance until external participation exists.
