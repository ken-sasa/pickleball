# PR-MDS v0.3 External Structured Review Protocol

**Status:** Open review protocol; no review has yet been completed.  
**Target:** 3-5 independent reviewers before journal submission if feasible; larger stakeholder consensus remains future work.

## 1. Purpose

The external review is designed to test whether PR-MDS v0.3 is understandable, minimally necessary, non-duplicative, and suitable for pilot testing. It is **not** a Delphi consensus process and does not convert v0.3 into a standard.

## 2. Reviewer domains

Seek independent review across at least three perspectives:

- sports injury epidemiology / sports medicine;
- research data standards, provenance, or open science;
- pickleball sport science, coaching, clinical practice, or field research.

Where possible, include a reviewer from outside the author's institution and a reviewer without prior collaboration with the author.

## 3. Conflict disclosure

Each reviewer should state:

- institutional relationship to the author;
- prior collaboration or co-authorship;
- financial or commercial interest in pickleball research, analytics, measurement, or standards;
- role in organizations that could benefit from adoption of PR-MDS;
- any other relationship the reviewer considers relevant.

A declared relationship does not automatically exclude a reviewer, but it must be visible in the review record.

## 4. Review questions

For each candidate **universal core field**, rate:

- Necessity for cross-study interpretation: 1 (not necessary) to 5 (essential)
- Definition clarity: 1 (ambiguous) to 5 (unambiguous)
- Collection/mapping burden: 1 (very high) to 5 (very low)
- International portability: 1 (poor) to 5 (strong)
- Preferred disposition: `retain_core`, `revise`, `move_to_module`, `replace_with_external_standard`, `remove`

Then answer:

1. Does the context-and-provenance framing solve a real problem not already solved by existing frameworks?
2. Which existing standards should PR-MDS reuse rather than redefine?
3. Which universal core fields are missing?
4. Which current core fields are unnecessary or too burdensome?
5. Are provenance classes mutually interpretable and sufficiently complete for pilot work?
6. Are any definitions unsafe, culturally narrow, or jurisdiction-dependent?
7. Does the v0.3 manuscript overstate evidence or consensus anywhere?
8. What is the single most important revision required before pilot testing?
9. Overall recommendation: `pilot_as_written`, `pilot_after_minor_revision`, `major_revision_before_pilot`, `do_not_pilot`.

## 5. Decision rule

No field is retained merely because reviewers average above an arbitrary threshold. Quantitative ratings are diagnostic. The author should publish a response matrix showing:

- reviewer comments;
- author response;
- change made or not made;
- rationale;
- any unresolved disagreement.

Where reviewers disagree materially, the disagreement should be preserved rather than averaged away.

## 6. Audit trail

Completed reviews should receive stable identifiers such as `EXT-REV-001`. The public record should preserve the version reviewed, date, disclosed interests, ratings, comments, and disposition of each issue, subject to reviewer consent for attribution.

## 7. Current status

- protocol published: **yes**
- invited reviewers: **0 recorded in repository at protocol creation**
- completed reviews: **0**
- consensus achieved: **no**

These values must not be changed until corresponding events actually occur.
