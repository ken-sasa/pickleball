# Zenodo Deposit Steps v0.2

1. Create a new Zenodo upload.
2. Upload `OPRC_Zenodo_Package_v0.2.zip` and the preprint PDF.
3. Apply the metadata in `ZENODO_DEPOSIT_METADATA_v0.2.json`.
4. Confirm the proposed **CC BY 4.0** license before publication.
5. Reserve a DOI while the record is still a draft.
6. Insert the reserved DOI into the manuscript's `Availability of Data and Materials` section and add a dataset citation to the reference list.
7. Rebuild or manually update the final manuscript and preprint.
8. Publish the Zenodo record, verify the DOI resolves, and only then submit the final files through the journal portal.

Do not describe the 28-record seed as an exhaustive or independently verified evidence synthesis.
